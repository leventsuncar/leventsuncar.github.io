Contact

How to play

Launch: drag your finger down to pull the slingshot, drag sideways to aim, then let go.
Steer: hold and drag. Left/right banks the glider, down lifts the nose, up dives.
Score: the closer you fly to the ground, cliffs and trees, the higher your multiplier (up to ×4).
Thermals: blue columns lift you up; orange ones throw you much higher, into eagle territory.

FAQ

When does the daily valley change?
Every day at 08:00 UTC. The daily leaderboard resets at the same time.

The leaderboard doesn't open.
Sign in to Game Center in your device's Settings → Game Center, then restart the game.

Can I unlock better gliders?
No. Everyone flies under the same conditions; only skill matters.
